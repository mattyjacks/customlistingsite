🎯 1. Core Strategy Overview

Primary Objective

Secure a 5-minute screen-share demo. Do not sell the product on the cold call; sell the meeting.

Cold Call Framework

2. Script Builder: The Standard Flow

Step 1: The Opener (Pattern Interrupt)

"Hey [Agent Name], this is Matty. I know I’m calling you completely out of the blue, so I’ll keep this to 30 seconds. Do you have a brief moment?"

Step 2: The Hook (The 'X-Factor')

"I run a development agency that builds high-end, standalone websites for individual properties. We’re finding that when agents show a seller their own dedicated site during a presentation, they win the listing over 90% of the time because it makes the seller feel like a VIP. Are you currently using standalone sites for your premium listings?"

Step 3: The Close (Low Friction)

"I’d love to take just 5 minutes next Tuesday to show you exactly how this looks. If you love it, we can talk about your next listing; if not, we part as friends. Do mornings or afternoons work better for you?"
Adaptive Pitching

3. Situational Branching

Scenario A

The Gatekeeper (Office Admin)

Goal: Gain info or a direct line.

"Hi, I'm putting together a custom property website demo for [Agent Name]. I imagine they're in the field right now—when is the best 60-second window to catch them at their desk, or is there a direct mobile number I can text the demo link to?"
Scenario B

The Top Producer (High Volume)

Angle: Efficiency and Luxury.

"A standard MLS link doesn't scream 'premium' for a million-dollar listing. I specialize in rapid development so you can offer elite marketing without adding any work to your plate. Let's do a 5-minute screen-share on Tuesday."
Scenario C

The New Agent (Hungry Competitor)

Angle: The Competitive Edge.

"When you’re up against veterans with 20 years of referrals, you need a shiny weapon. Sliding an iPad across the table and showing a dedicated property site levels the playing field instantly. I'll show you the template next week."
Scenario D

"Market is Too Fast" Objection

Angle: Future Lead Gen.

"You're right, the house will sell itself. This isn't for the current house—it's for the neighbors. When they see this premium site, you're the agent they call to list their home next. It turns one sale into three."
Overcoming Pushback

4. Objection Handling

🗣️ "Just email me."
"Happy to. To ensure it's relevant, what's your biggest frustration with listing presentations right now? I'll tailor the demo to that."
🗣️ "Brokerage provides this."
"Brokerage pages look like every other listing. I build standalone assets that highlight the lifestyle of the home. It’s a completely different tier of marketing."
🗣️ "No Budget."
"That's why I want to show it to you. Most agents use this to justify their commission. It’s an investment that pays for itself in one listing."
