Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026
State Jurisdiction: New Hampshire, USA (RSA 507-H)
Operator: MattyJacks.com

1. Overview & New Hampshire Statutory Compliance

At MattyJacks.com (operating single-property showcase websites including CustomListingSite), we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, disclosure, and safeguard data when you interact with our single-property listing websites, schedule property showings, or request custom website creation services.

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply directly with the statutory obligations established under the New Hampshire Data Privacy Act (NHDPA, NH RSA 507-H) as well as applicable federal regulations. We do not sell your personal data to data brokers or unauthorized third parties.

2. Infrastructure & Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver fast, secure, and resilient web services, our website utilizes infrastructure provided by leading cloud and security vendors:

Vercel Inc. (Web Hosting)

Our web application and static assets are hosted on Vercel Inc. Vercel processes HTTP requests, server logs, IP metadata, and edge routing to ensure high-speed global availability and uptime.

Cloudflare, Inc. (Registrar & Security)

Our domain names are registered with Cloudflare, Inc. as our domain registrar. Cloudflare acts as our DNS provider, Content Delivery Network (CDN), and Web Application Firewall (WAF) to protect against DDoS attacks and encrypt traffic via TLS/SSL.

These service providers access technical connection logs solely to execute operational routing, security telemetry, and platform delivery on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.

3. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal information in two ways depending on how you interact with our site:

  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: When you fill out forms on our website (such as scheduling a property walkthrough or ordering a property website), we may collect your full name, email address, telephone number, agency affiliation, and property preferences.
  • Automatically Collected Technical Data: When visiting our website, Vercel and Cloudflare automatically log standard HTTP request metadata including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, time of access, and pages viewed.

4. Purposes of Data Processing

We process your personal data strictly for legitimate business purposes including:

  • Fulfilling requests for single-property tour showings and contacting listing agents.
  • Processing website creation orders and delivering custom preview links.
  • Maintaining site security, mitigating cyber threats, and verifying DDoS protection through Cloudflare.
  • Complying with applicable New Hampshire laws and legal obligations.

5. Your Consumer Privacy Rights (NH RSA 507-H)

Under the New Hampshire Data Privacy Act, residents of New Hampshire possess specific statutory rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Confirm & Access: You have the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and access a copy of such data.
  • Right to Correct: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of personal data provided by or obtained about you.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to obtain your personal data in a portable, readily usable format.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You have the right to opt-out of the processing of your personal data for targeted advertising, sale, or profiling. (Note: MattyJacks.com does not sell personal data).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your statutory privacy rights.

6. Data Security & Retention

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards — including TLS encryption enforced through Cloudflare and secure Vercel deployments — to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of your personal data. Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to meet legal and record-keeping mandates.
