Architectural Masterpiece

Where Rustic New Hampshire Elegance Meets Modern Luxury

Nestled along 220 feet of pristine shoreline on Lake Winnipesaukee, The Pinnacle represents the pinnacle of New Hampshire lakefront architecture. Built with locally quarried granite and native Douglas fir timber framing, this mastercrafted sanctuary offers floor-to-ceiling lakeside views, a professional chef's kitchen, and an expansive covered balcony for breathtaking sunset gatherings.

Every detail was engineered for luxury, comfort, and year-round mountain lifestyle entertainment.

Gourmet Chef's Kitchen & Dining ▼
Features custom walnut cabinetry, Calacatta marble waterfall island, Sub-Zero dual refrigerator tower, Wolf 6-burner gas range, walk-in butler pantry, and wine cellar.
Primary Master Sanctuary & Balcony ▼
Vaulted wood beam ceilings, private covered stone balcony, gas fireplace, spa bath with free-standing soaking tub overlooking the lake, and radiant warm stone flooring.
Outdoor Living & Dock Facilities ▼
Heated bluestone patio with gas fire pit, custom mahogany boat dock with deep water slip, jet-ski lifts, outdoor kitchen, and private sandy beach access.
