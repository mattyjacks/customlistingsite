Architectural Narrative

Welcome to 77 Example Road in desirable Chester, NH! Presented by Example Realty, this exquisite 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath residence offers modern architectural elegance on 2.5 private acres.

The home features a functional open-concept layout with light-filled living space, vaulted beam ceilings, and premium hardwood flooring. The gourmet kitchen is outfitted with custom cabinetry, quartz waterfall island, gas range, and butler's pantry.

Enjoy peaceful wooded surroundings with expansive outdoor patio, 2-car garage, and top-tier Rockingham County school district access.
