About This Home

Welcome to 42 Lakeview Ridge Rd, Unit B in desirable Meredith, NH! This modest 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath condominium offers an affordable gateway to the Lakes Region.

The unit features a functional layout with an open living space, neutral grey painted drywall, and wall-to-wall carpeting. The kitchen is outfitted with custom slate-blue cabinetry, blue mosaic tile countertops and backsplash, gas range, and double stainless steel sink.

Enjoy peaceful wooded surroundings with outdoor walkway bridge entry, reserved asphalt parking, and low-maintenance condo association care covering water, trash, and exterior maintenance. Conveniently located near local shops, dining, and public boat launches on Lake Winnipesaukee.
